Vehicle / 01 · Beach Launch Team

Theseus

Avionics Member · Electrical harnessing, instrumentation and checkout

ROLE
Avionics Member

PERIOD
January – April 2026

Theseus was Beach Launch Team’s liquid bi-propellant rocket, burning kerosene and liquid oxygen (LOX) through an LR-101 engine. I worked on its electrical interfaces, including solenoid valve, pressure transducer and power-line harnessing. I also built a custom DB37 harness for the LabJack T7 Pro, checked pressure readings through the LabJack GUI, tested solenoid actuation with the team’s relay switchbox, and assembled a 24 V to 5 V converter board for the LabJack module.

Harnessing & connectors

Wired solenoid valves, pressure transducers and power lines. Soldered and crimped secure Deutsch, EC5 and Molex connectors.

  • Made a custom DB37 harness for the LabJack T7 Pro.

02 / Checkout

Instrumentation & actuation

Connected the harnesses to the control module and checked the sensor and valve paths.

  • Read pressure transducer data with the LabJack GUI.
  • Tested solenoid actuation through a relay switchbox wired to the harnesses.

24 V to 5 V converter

Soldered a buck converter onto a custom perfboard with screw-terminal hookups.

  • Provided a 5 V USB output from the 24 V power line.
  • Prepared the output to power the LabJack module in flight.

Student-run rocketry project · Schedule I

Avionics co-lead

Remote operations, avionics control and electrical integration

STATUS
Work in progress

STARTED
August 2026

Schedule I aims to establish a framework other collegiate rocketry projects can build on, including remote control of procedures such as LOX fill using solenoid-actuated ball valves. The project’s goal is to improve operational safety by reducing time spent near the rocket, especially during LOX fill, while streamlining teams’ work at launch sites. These are project goals; the control system and electrical integration described here are still in development.

01 / Control board

Avionics PCB design

I’m designing a Power/Control PCB to control solenoid valve actuation, servo operation, and current channels for ignition circuitry.

  • Solenoid valve actuation is controlled through MOSFET gate drivers.
  • Digital PWM signals from our STM32 microcontroller pass through op-amp buffer stages for servo operation.
  • MOSFETs and current-sense comparators support ignition-circuit arming and current measurement.
02 / System integration

DB37 electrical interface

The board is being designed around a DB37 connection to integrate with the broader electrical system.

  • System interfaces include separate power-distribution and instrumentation boards.
  • Instrumentation includes thermocouples and pressure transducers.
03 / In progress

Connectors & harnesses

Alongside the PCB design, I’m sourcing connectors and preparing wiring harnesses for the system.

  • Preparing the electrical interconnects for board-to-board integration.
  • Supporting a cohesive avionics system across control, power and instrumentation.
